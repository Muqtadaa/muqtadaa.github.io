Agdaron is one world among several, and the campaign has spent its later years
outside it. These are the places the party can actually get to, and what is
waiting in each.

## The Feywild

Reached through the Arc du Fey, among other ways, and the reason nobody sensible
uses it twice.

Time runs differently there. It is not a fixed ratio and it is not fair: a
season spent in the Feywild can cost decades on the far side, and the party has
come back to find people they met as children grown old.

Politics there run on the seasons. Four courts — Summer, Winter, Spring and
Autumn — with Summer and Winter as the powers and the other two as the ground
they fight over. Summer and Winter had been locked into the same stalemate for
generations, which suited both of their rulers and nobody else.

The Feywild also has older things buried under it than the courts care to
discuss, including a tomb that predates all four of them.

## The Astral Sea

The Astral Sea connects the planes, and it is a sea in every way that matters:
you need a ship, you need a crew, and the things living in it treat you as
weather.

What sails there: **Githyanki** and **Githzerai**, who have hated each other
long enough that the hatred is load-bearing. Astral dreadnoughts. Astral sharks,
which eat souls. Ethereal filaments that drift and feed on psychic energy.
Astral storms that damage the mind rather than the hull. Rogue wizards, other
adventurers, and pirates.

The pirates are worth their own paragraph. **Leviathan** is a civilisation built
on and inside the skull of a dead cosmic serpent, and it is the closest thing
the Astral Sea has to a port you can trust, which is not very close.

Somewhere in it, the **Ruby Gate** burns like a wound. It is the way into Hell,
and it is a two-way door — which is how a dwarven nobleman went through it and
came out the other side a Devil Prince.

## The Nine Hells

A machine for punishment, built by Asmodeus, running without an off switch and
without a release valve. Nothing in it is designed to rehabilitate anyone. That
observation is the campaign's central moral question, and the party has been
chewing on it since the first layer.

- **1. Avernus** — Zariel. A barren wasteland of fiery pits and the damned,
  and currently a warfront: Zariel and the Order of the Cleansing Flame against
  forces loyal to Asmodeus.
- **2. Dis** — Dispater. A city of iron and fire, and the place where Hell stops
  being a landscape and becomes an administration. Souls in ledgers, tolls
  between districts, True Names bound into infernal record. The dangerous devils
  here prefer signatures to swords.
- **3. Minauros** — Glasya. A vast rotting swamp, a twisted mockery of Melorah's
  domain, and Glasya is Melorah's dark reflection: an archdruid of spores with
  poisons worked into the ground itself.
- **4. Phlegethos** — Geryon. A gloomy desert of jagged rock and searing
  sandstorms, where the ruler bends reality and the landscape lies. Mirage
  cities, labyrinths of shifting dunes, oases that poison you.
- **5. Stygia** — Cimeries. A frozen city. The damned are set into the ice in
  poses that match their sins, and the lord of the layer can animate them.
- **6. Mulciberus** — Tiamat. A sea of fire hot enough to melt steel, crossed by
  burning ships. The damned burn in the lava, and can be used as a shield.
- **7. Maladomini** — Andrealphus. A carnival of horrors: rides built to
  torment, performers who guard them, and a ringmaster who would rather turn a
  party against itself than fight it.
- **8. Cocytus** — An underground mine full of precious metals and gems, worked
  by demonic miners and deadly machinery. Its devil lord was not born to the
  title. He bought it with a soul he did not have to sell.
- **9. Nessus** — Asmodeus. The bottom. Towering infernos, rivers of lava, and
  the throne. Asmodeus made the Hellfire Crown, and he is holding Malyr, God of
  Battle, prisoner here.

The rest of the cosmology exists and the party has touched most of it in
passing: the Shadowfell, the Elemental Chaos, the Abyss, Limbo, Arvandor and the
House of Nature. None of it has needed a map yet.
