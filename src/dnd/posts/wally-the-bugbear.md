Wally is a bugbear and, by the reckoning of his own people, a teenager. He is a
Junior Grub Scout of the Hobgoblin Dominion of Hashara, which is a real rank
with real badges, and he will explain both at length to anyone who does not
physically leave.

He is a shaman. The connection to nature is genuine and surprisingly deep for
someone who has been alive for so little of it. The connection to the concept of
personal space is not there at all.

He is the most-drawn character in this collection, which is what happens when a
dungeon master finally gets to sit on the other side of the screen. The portrait
above is the first proper one; the related art below is the running record.

Hashara is not in Agdaron, and Wally has never met a Veil Piercer. He would like
to.
