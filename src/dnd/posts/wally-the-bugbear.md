For the first time in five years I am on the other side of the screen: not
running the campaign, not voicing a thousand and one NPCs, but playing one
character. Wally is a bugbear, a teenager by his people's reckoning, and a
Junior Grub Scout of the Hobgoblin Dominion of Hashara. He is a shaman with a
deep connection to nature and almost no connection to the concept of personal
space.

He has been drawn more than any other character at the table, which is what
happens when the DM finally gets to play. The related art below is the running
record; the portrait at the top of this post is the first proper one.
