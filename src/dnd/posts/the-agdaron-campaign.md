The first campaign in Agdaron ran for years, long enough that the map got
redrawn twice and the party outlived a couple of villains who were supposed to
be the whole plot.

What I want to write down here is the story as it was actually played, rather
than the one I planned: the mid-game session where the players stopped
following the quest board, the shopkeeper who was meant to have one scene and
became the party's moral compass, and the natural twenty at the worst possible
moment. Those are the bits anyone still brings up.
