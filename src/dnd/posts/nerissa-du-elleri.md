The betrothal was explained to her as arithmetic. Elleria had not won the war.
Malinar had not quite won it either. A marriage would bind the two crowns
together, guarantee Ellerian autonomy on paper, and cost the kingdom one
daughter.

The introduction dinner was set for half past eight.

Nerissa was not at it.

She went down through the Court District under cloud cover on a Friday night,
into the Lower District where the labourers were getting off work and nobody
looks twice at a hood, and out of the city entirely with five strangers she had
met that evening. The guard captain who nearly caught her on the stairs ended up
travelling with her for the next several years.

## What she is good at

Everything a court teaches and nothing it intends to. She reads a room for who
is nervous and why. She gets into places that are locked, and out of
conversations that are worse. When a fight starts she is rarely where she was
standing a moment ago, and when it goes badly she is the one who disengages and
comes back at a better angle.

The information she is best at extracting is the kind nobody thinks they are
giving away. In Malinar it was her, at a feast, who worked out that the Crown
Prince had an illegitimate sister held hostage by the Paladin Order, a father
under the same guard, and a court jester willing to die for him. All three
turned out to matter enormously.

## What it has cost

Her mother was murdered by Direll Lazlow, celebrated hero of Malinar and one of
the architects of the coup that destroyed it. Nerissa was in the catacombs when
he died.

Her father spent much of the campaign missing, in the company of an arch-wizard
named Ebrin Jementh. When they finally found one another again, she sat with him
in an inn and turned his hair grey with an account of the intervening year.

In Malinar a banishment threw her out of the world entirely. She came back
through a plane of light and crystal, the personal outer realm of the Goddess of
Light, where Althea's old worshippers still enjoy an afterlife in an unending
city square. Nerissa walked their streets, climbed a tower full of everything
they had ever known, took a Ring of Revivify and a shard of glowing crystal, and
used the shard as a key to open a door into the Nexus.

She is the only member of the party to have seen the Gray Lady's heaven from the
inside.

She was not impressed.

## Where she is now

Aboard the Gray Lady's Peril, descending through the Nine Hells, some distance
from the dinner she walked out of.
