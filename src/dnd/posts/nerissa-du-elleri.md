Nerissa du Elleri is the daughter of the High King of Elleria.

Her betrothal to Malinar was an instrument of state. Elleria had not won the
war. A marriage would bind the two crowns together, guarantee Ellerian autonomy
on paper, and cost the kingdom one daughter.

She was not consulted.

The introduction dinner was set for half past eight. She left the Court District
under cloud cover, went down into the Lower District where the labourers were
coming off work, and was out of the city before the guard understood she was
gone. She left with five strangers she had met that evening. One of them was the
guard captain who had nearly stopped her on the stairs.

What the court taught her, she has used against courts. She reads a room for who
is nervous and why. She opens locks, and she gets out of conversations that are
more dangerous than the locks. Some of that is training. The rest is psionic,
and it does not announce itself.

At the champions' feast in Malinar she learned in a single evening that the
Crown Prince had an illegitimate sister held hostage by the Paladin Order, that
his father was under the same guard, and that his court jester would die for
him. All three proved decisive.

Direll Lazlow murdered her mother. Nerissa was in the catacombs beneath Malinar
when he died.

Her father spent most of the campaign missing, in the company of the arch-wizard
Ebrin Jementh. When the two of them found each other again, she sat with him in
an inn and turned his hair grey with an account of the year.

A banishment in Malinar threw her out of the world entirely. She came back
through a plane of light and crystal: the private outer realm of the Goddess of
Light, where Althea's old worshippers still keep an afterlife in an unending
city square. Nerissa walked their streets. She climbed a tower that held
everything they had ever known. She took a Ring of Revivify and a shard of
glowing crystal, and used the shard as a key to open a door into the Nexus.

She is the only member of the party to have seen the Gray Lady's heaven from the
inside.
