Peacetime did not suit him.

He was in the city watch office in Elleris, being asked by its commander to keep
half an eye on a restless capital, when the evening produced a runaway princess
on a staircase and a Malinari squad leaning over a barkeep. He handled both. He
never went back to the watch.

Murdock is described in the records as stoic and dutiful, carrying justice in
his bones and silence in his steps. Both halves are accurate. He is also the
member of the party most likely to grapple something and hold it still until the
problem resolves.

## The god and the church

Malyr is God of Battle and Might. He is also God of Justice and Defense, and the
Church that ruled Malinar had spent twenty years quietly editing the second half
out.

Murdock is sworn to the Oath of Devotion: honesty, courage, compassion, honour,
duty. It is not a complicated list, and by the time the Veil Piercers reached
Malinar the Paladin Order had abandoned every item on it while wearing the same
symbol.

Murdock worked this out slowly and then all at once. Banished mid-fight from a
Malinari street, he found himself looking directly at Malyr, and in that moment
he was finally able to separate the god he had sworn to from the tyrant the
Paladin Order had built in his name.

Then the Church launched its coup, and the distinction stopped being theological.

## What it cost him

In the royal keep, with the fighting already lost, Murdock learned that the king
dying on the floor was his father. That Crown Prince Rami, whom he had just
fought beside, was his half-brother. That the hostage the Paladin Order had been
using as leverage on both of them was his sister Isabelle.

All three of King Ramsey's illegitimate children turned out to be siblings of
one another.

He was too late for his father. He was not too late for Rami.

Then Malyr's own avatar came down on the capital and burned it.

## Where he went

South to Dolonde, with the rest of them, through a vampiric conspiracy and up a
mountain a goddess raised out of the ground. He pinned Magnus Rex in place with
his bare hands while the weapons came online.

When the survivors fled the city, Murdock stayed.

He had gathered the first followers of a New Church of Malyr — one built on the
belief that strength exists in service to justice rather than conquest — and
somebody had to be there to build it.

He is still in Dolonde, doing that.
