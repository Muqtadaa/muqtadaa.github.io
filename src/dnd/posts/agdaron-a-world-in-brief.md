Agdaron started as a map. Before there was a campaign, a pantheon or a single
named NPC, there was a sheet of parchment-coloured canvas in Procreate with a
coastline, a mountain range that took a whole evening, and a compass rose I was
far too proud of. The world grew outward from wherever the party decided to
walk next.

## The shape of the place

This is where the geography goes: the continents, the seas between them, and
the handful of regions the players have actually seen. A good rule for this
section is to describe only what a traveller could learn in a tavern; the
secrets belong in the campaign posts.

## Who runs things

Factions, crowns and churches. The Hobgoblin Dominion of Hashara is the one
everybody has heard of, because Wally came from it. The rest are still notes in
the margin of the DM screen.

## The rules of the world

The bits of homebrew that change how the game plays: how magic is taught, what
the gods actually answer, which monsters are real and which are stories told to
keep children out of the woods. Keep this short; if it needs a table, it needs
its own post.

## What the map gets wrong

Every campaign redraws something. This is the running list of places the party
has renamed, burned down or accidentally founded, so the next version of the
map can catch up.
