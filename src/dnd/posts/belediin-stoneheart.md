House Stoneheart once held ten floors of manor carved into the wall of Barazar
by the founders of the Underkeep, and a sword that meant something when it was
drawn.

By Belediin's generation the manor had been sold. The family lived in a
three-storey townhouse in the common residential district, and the sword was a
story his grandfather told. The dwarf leading the merchant caravan out of
Elleris called him "my lord" and meant it as an insult.

Belediin went looking for the blade.

He has no magic of his own, and he is not fast. The rogues and the wizard leave
him behind in every chase the campaign has run. What he does is stand where the
party needs someone to stand, and very little gets past him.

Everything divine about him is borrowed, and all of it is borrowed from one
object.

The Sword of the Stonehearts came back to him in the ruins of Caer Morghaen. Its
sheath did not. That went north on the back of a possessed dwarven child. When
he spends the blade's charges to raise Spirit Guardians at full strength, what
answers is the shade of the great-grandfather who lost the sword in the first
place.

He met Arielle Coldaine in a vision before he met her in the flesh: a mute
dwarven girl in great distress, wearing his family's crest on a sheath across
her back. He comforted her. Weeks later he watched a fallen goddess open her
eyes inside that child's body. He has spent every year since trying to get her
out of it.

On the mountain Althea raised beneath the party in Dolonde, Belediin contracted
vampirism. He built a coffin to sleep in, and spent a season in the Feywild
bargaining with fey royalty for a cure. He got one.

In a temple in Dolonde he knelt at an altar with nothing to offer but his own
body, and swore to Morudin that he would accept any affliction laid upon him so
long as it did not leave his friends or the world weaker for his weakness, and
that he would carry the Stonesword against every wrong he had witnessed for as
long as he could lift it.

Morudin was pleased.

His father is not missing. His father fell through the Ruby Gate into the Astral
Sea and is a Devil Prince in Hell. His mother is imprisoned there.

Belediin is on his way down.
